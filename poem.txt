Little Butterfly and Little Bee

I know a little butterfly and a little bee with tiny golden wings,
He plays among the summer flowers and up and down he swings,
He dances on their honey cups so happy all the day,
And then he spreads his tiny wings—and softly flies away.

But when the sun begins to set and stars begin to gleam,
He folds his tiny golden wings and has a quiet dream,
He rests upon a velvet leaf until the morning light,
Then wakes to dance among the flowers, beautiful and bright.

He sips the morning dew so sweet and dances with the breeze,
He races with the bumblebees and plays among the trees,
A drift of living color floating softly through the air,
A little touch of magic spreading wonder everywhere.
